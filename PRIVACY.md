# Privacy Policy for Enable CP

Last updated: November 2026

**Enable CP** is a browser extension that restores copy, paste, right-click and text selection on websites that block them, and lets you copy text from pictures ("Snip to copy").

## What data we collect
**None.** Enable CP does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal information or browsing data.

## How it works with your data
- Text you copy, paste or snip stays on your device. It is only placed on your own clipboard.
- Text recognition (OCR) for pictures runs entirely inside your browser using files included in the extension. Screenshots taken for "Snip to copy" are processed in memory on your device and are never uploaded.
- The only setting saved is the on/off switch of the extension, stored locally in your browser.
- The extension makes **no network requests** and uses no remote code, analytics or advertising.

## Permissions
- *Access to all websites*: needed because blocked copy and paste can happen on any website, and to take the screenshot used by "Snip to copy". Nothing from these sites is sent anywhere.
- *storage*: saves the on/off switch.
- *clipboardWrite*: puts the text you copy or snip on your clipboard.
- *offscreen*: runs the on-device text recognition in a hidden page.
